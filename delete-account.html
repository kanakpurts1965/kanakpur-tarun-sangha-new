Account Deletion Request

Club Accounts Management System

Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting our support team using the email address provided below.

Important: Sending a request does not automatically delete your account. Your identity may need to be verified before the request can be processed.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Send an email to the support address below.
  3. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include your registered name and registered email address or phone number.
  5. Our team will verify your request and communicate the next steps.

Contact Support

Send your account deletion request to:

tanmoyadak112@gmail.com

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the account and applicable requirements, deletion may include account information, contact details, authentication records, and other personal data associated with the account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain financial, accounting, transaction, security, or legally required records may need to be retained where applicable. Retained information should be limited to what is necessary and handled according to applicable requirements.

Processing and Confirmation

After receiving your request, our team will verify the request and inform you about the next steps. Account deletion will be confirmed after the request has actually been processed.
