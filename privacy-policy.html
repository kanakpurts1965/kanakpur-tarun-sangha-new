Privacy Policy

App: Club Accounts Management System

Developer: Tanmoy Adak

Effective date: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Club Accounts Management System collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized users access the application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how the application is used, information may include:

  • Member names, contact details, and profile information entered into the app.
  • Account identifiers used for authentication and access control.
  • Club financial records, including credit, debit, contributions, contracts, statements, and program-related records.
  • Documents or agreement copies uploaded by authorized users.
  • Technical information generated when the app operates, where available through the services it uses.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users and manage authorized access.
  • Maintain club accounts, contributions, expenses, and reports.
  • Display relevant records to authorized administrators and members.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application.
  • Meet applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

4. Storage and Service Providers

The application uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for authentication and data storage. Information may be processed by these service providers as necessary to operate the application and provide their services.

5. Access and Sharing

Information may be accessible to authorized club administrators and users according to their assigned permissions. Information may also be processed by service providers supporting the application or disclosed when required by law. Personal information is not sold to third parties.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for club administration, accounting, record-keeping, and applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the developer using the details below. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate accounting or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for authorized club administration and membership activities, not specifically for children. Please contact the developer if you believe information has been provided inappropriately.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its information practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate.
